About

I'm a senior mechanical engineering student at the University of Georgia, concentrating on propulsion and aerospace systems. I currently lead the UGA AIAA Design, Build, Fly propulsion team, am finishing my private pilot certificate, and work hands-on with real engines and airframes as an A&P apprentice.

SEC. 02

Projects

Designing, building, and breaking is where I've grown the most in my engineering career. This is where coursework turns into real intuition, and where I've built the skillset to actually call myself an engineer.

2026 UGA DBF Plane Landing Gear

Designed, simulated, and manufactured the landing gear for our 2026 AIAA DBF aircraft, sizing its stroke to absorb touchdown loads within the elastic range of 6061 aluminum — flew the entire competition season without a failure.

CNC Machined Coolant Elbow

A CNC-machined replacement coolant elbow after the factory part cracked from decades of thermal cycling and couldn't be TIG welded cleanly — modeled and machined from 6061 aluminum on a mill and lathe across five setups.

FSAE Formula Car Smart Steering Wheel

Led FSAE's smart steering wheel program for two seasons — nine driver-tested prototypes, an FEA-verified 7075 aluminum structural plate, and a live data display that included paddle shifters for 2026.

1991 Honda ST1100 Restoration

Trailered home non-running after sitting from 2005 to 2026, then diagnosed and rebuilt every faulty system — fuel, ignition, carburetors, and 3 separate hydraulic circuits — sourcing rare parts internationally. Now registered and running like it did from the factory.

ST1100 Carburetor Rebuild and Intake Manifold Synchronization

Pulling and rebuilding all four CV carburetors on the ST1100, then synchronizing the intake manifolds so the V4 idles and pulls evenly across all cylinders.
